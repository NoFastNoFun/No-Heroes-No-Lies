Game start:

Each player get a random hero card.
2 monster from the monster deck get put on the active monsters.

Random player or last session's winner starts his round:

Player first declare his "identity" (can be fake), can be his true card or a false, other player get an option to accuse current playing player that he is a liar.
This accuse option doesn't stop the gameplay current playing can continue but his action will be stopped if he was indeed lying.
In the meantime active player can either:

- Pick a card, then discard either the picked card or his current card, then use a free or paid power
- Attack a monster
- If 6 gems in his pocket he can try to demask another player's true hero card, if right the accused loose his card and a life.

All declared "identity" (can be fake) passive must run. For instance if a player lastly declared that he is a Knight and that current player slays a monster he will earn
the gems.

Turn is finished we go to the next player.

Game ends if only 1 player is alive, A player has a sum of coins equaling the amount of players + 1.

Last discarded card must always be visible until a new card is discarded.
