Game start:

Burn 1 random hero card (burn means perma lost for the game session)
Each player get a random hero card.
2 monster from the monster deck get put on the active monsters.

Game starts when game master (player who created the lobby) decides to start the game AND all player slots are filled

Random player or last session's winner starts his round:

- Pick a card, then discard either the picked card or his current card, then declare identity (can be fake) then use a free or paid power, knowing that paid power also triggers first the free power.
- Attack a monster, declare identity then select one of the two active monsters, if strength modifier of "identity" is stronger than monster, gain monster loot, otherwise loose a life
- If 6 gems in his pocket, another option is given to player, he can try to demask another player's true hero card, if right the accused loose his card and a life, this option doesn't require player to declare his identity compared to the other two options.

All declared "identity" (can be fake) passive must run. For instance if a player lastly declared that he is a Knight and that current player slays a monster he will earn the gems.

Turn is finished automatically once the action and passive have runned, we then go to the next player.

Game ends if only 1 player is alive, A player has a sum of coins equaling the amount of players + 1.

Last discarded card must always be visible until a new card is discarded.
